Traffic in the drive estimates
How to read this. Two ways to look. Near schools: pick a level and a minimum rating, and the map is shaded by the drive to the nearest school that passes. From a place: click the map to see the drive to the three schools that spot is zoned for and to nearby private schools; tick "All schools" to add every school that passes your filters. Drive times are estimates from a simple road-speed model (highways, main roads, local streets, lakes as barriers). They're not live traffic. Expect ±25%, and check your shortlist in Google Maps at the hour you'd drive.

Neighborhood outlines come from the City of Austin Community Registry (neighborhood and homeowner association areas) and the US Census (West Lake Hills, Rollingwood, Lost Creek, Barton Creek, Steiner Ranch). "Westlake" is the whole Eanes ISD area, which takes in West Lake Hills, Rollingwood and the parts of Austin around them. Published neighborhoods leave gaps, so Census ZIP code areas sit underneath and every home falls in at least one area. ★ marks the six focus areas. None of these are legal or school boundaries. Satellite imagery: Esri World Imagery.

School zones: Austin ISD's 2026-27 school assignments and Leander ISD's 2024-25 attendance zones, from the districts' own maps. Eanes ISD's zones come from the district's own address lookup (2026-27). Lake Travis, Round Rock and Lago Vista ISDs publish no usable zone data, so their areas have no zones. Click anywhere on the map to see the schools that spot is zoned for. Confirm an address with the district before relying on it.

Schools are every public campus (district and charter) in the area, with location and A to F rating from the Texas Education Agency's TXschools.gov feed. Their student counts are the agency's October 2024 enrollment; private schools' counts are from the federal survey. A school with no count shows only under "Any size". Private schools come from the federal Private School Universe Survey (2023-24, with 2021-22 filling in schools that skipped a round); schools report themselves, so the list can miss some (a few that Niche grades but the survey lacks are added, placed by the US Census geocoder), and the state does not rate them. Where Niche gives a private school an overall grade (its 2027 Austin-area private school list), that grade stands in for the rating. Public schools' Niche grades are not loaded yet: Niche blocks automated reading, so each school links to its Niche page instead.
